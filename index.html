Description

Schnittger Bourne is pleased to present 17 Rossmore Drive to the market.

A superbly presented home that has been thoughtfully extended and upgraded to create a bright, contemporary living space, this property perfectly suited to modern family life.

Situated within a quiet cul-de-sac in this highly regarded and well-maintained neighbourhood, No. 17 combines generous accommodation with stylish interiors, close to every local amenity.

Externally, the property benefits from a cobblelock driveway providing off-street parking for two vehicles, mature planting, and an inviting front elevation that immediately creates an excellent first impression.

Within, accommodation briefly comprises a bright hallway, iimpressive open-plan kitchen, dining and family room, utility, reception room, master bedroom with en suite, three guest bedrooms, main bathroom, WC and HP.

The heart of this home is without doubt the extended kitchen and living space. It is flooded with natural light from overhead rooflights and expansive glazing overlooking the private, low maintenance rear garden.

The contemporary shaker-style kitchen offers excellent storage and generous worktop space, while the spacious dining area comfortably accommodates large gatherings. Off the kitchen is a practically laid out utility room.

To the front of the property, the welcoming reception room provides a cosy retreat with open fireplace, while upstairs the generous bedrooms offer flexibility for for residents, guests and those working from home.

Location:

Rossmore residents enjoy the convenience of being within walking distance of local schools, cafés, supermarkets, sporting facilities and the village centre, while the N11/M11 provides effortless access to Greystones, Bray, Wicklow Town and Dublin. The surrounding area also offers an abundance of scenic walks, beaches, golf courses and the spectacular Wicklow countryside.
